Ancient Imperial History

Kyoto served as Japan's imperial capital for over a millennium. Founded in 794 AD as Heian-kyo, the city remains the cultural heart of traditional Japanese heritage, surviving centuries of transformation with its grand shrines and classic architecture.

Rich Traditions & Culture

From centuries-old tea ceremonies to exquisite Kaiseki gastronomy and the serene atmosphere of Gion's geisha quarters, Kyoto provides an unparalleled window into Japan's refined aesthetic traditions.

Iconic Landmarks & Temples

Home to thousands of Buddhist temples and Shinto shrines, iconic sites like Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion), Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, and Fushimi Inari-taisha captivate travelers from around the globe.
